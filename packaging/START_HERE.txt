HATUA - START HERE
==================

Hatua helps Kenyan teachers and parents follow each learner from Grade 4 to Grade 9.
It runs on this computer. Learner data never goes to the internet.

1. OPEN HATUA
   Mac:     Open the Hatua app. The first time, right-click it and choose Open,
            then press Open again. (Hatua is free and not from the App Store,
            so the Mac asks once.)
   Windows: Open the Hatua folder and double-click Hatua.exe. If Windows says
            "Windows protected your PC", press "More info", then "Run anyway".

2. A SMALL HATUA WINDOW OPENS, AND HATUA OPENS IN YOUR WEB BROWSER.
   The first time it makes a practice school with made-up people (20 seconds).

3. SIGN IN WITH A PRACTICE ACCOUNT
   Teacher: M-6001    Parent: P-100001    PIN: 246810

4. THE AI HELPER (QWEN) - OPTIONAL
   On the sign-in page, follow the steps in the box "Add the AI helper".
   You install Ollama once (free). Then Hatua downloads Qwen for you
   (about 4.7 GB, once). Hatua works without it too.

5. READ THE GUIDE
   Open "User guide.html" in this folder, or press Help inside Hatua.

To stop Hatua, close the small Hatua window.
